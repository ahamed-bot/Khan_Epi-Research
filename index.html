ABOUT

Understanding how chromatin regulation shapes plant biology.

I am a plant molecular biologist interested in the evolutionary and molecular principles underlying chromatin regulation in plants.

My research connects evolutionary biology, molecular biology, epigenetics, chromatin biology, genomics and computational approaches to investigate how regulatory systems emerge, diversify and function.

A major focus of my work is the evolution and function of PWWP-DOMAIN INTERACTOR OF POLYCOMBS (PWO) proteins, their association with Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2), and their interaction with TRB proteins in plant chromatin regulation.

I am also interested in how chromatin regulatory networks influence genome organisation, developmental plasticity and plant adaptation.

RESEARCH

Evolutionary epigenetics of plant chromatin

My research seeks to connect molecular mechanisms with evolutionary history and biological function.

01

Evolution of chromatin regulators

Reconstructing the emergence and diversification of chromatin regulatory proteins across plant lineages and eukaryotes.

02

PWO and Polycomb regulation

Understanding how PWO proteins interact with Polycomb-associated machinery and contribute to chromatin regulation and plant development.

03

Chromatin regulatory networks

Investigating molecular interactions between chromatin-associated proteins, DNA and regulatory complexes.

04

Genome organisation

Exploring how chromatin states and regulatory networks contribute to genome architecture and nuclear organisation.

05

Development and adaptation

Studying how chromatin regulation contributes to developmental programmes and plant responses to environmental conditions.

06

Comparative and evolutionary biology

Using comparative approaches to understand how regulatory innovation has shaped plant chromatin systems throughout evolution.

RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Evolution, epigenetic regulation and plant development

My work explores the relationship between the evolutionary diversification of chromatin regulators, their molecular interactions and their consequences for plant development and genome regulation.
